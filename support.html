Moving to a new phone

Your entries are stored only on your phone and are kept out of iCloud and device backups, so they do not move by themselves.

  • On the old phone: open the page icon at the top of the main screen, then Save a backup file. Save it to Files, a drive, or send it to yourself.
  • On the new phone: install Acclimate, tap I have a backup file on the first screen, then Restore from a backup.

We cannot recover entries for you: we never receive them.

Does Acclimate tell me what dose to take?

No. You enter the dose and the schedule your prescriber gave you. Acclimate counts what you log; it does not suggest doses or dates and does not give medical advice.

What "scheduled times with no entry" means

It counts the times on your own schedule that passed without a logged dose near them. Acclimate only knows what was logged, not what happened. A weekly dose logged up to three and a half days early or late still counts for its week.

Reminders do not arrive

Open Settings on your iPhone, then Notifications, then Acclimate, and allow notifications. In Acclimate, open the gear icon and switch the reminder on. A reminder is skipped on a day you have already logged.

Apple Health

Sharing is optional and covers body weight only. Switch it on or off with the gear icon in Acclimate, or in the Health app under Sharing › Apps.

Subscription

  • To cancel or change plans: iPhone Settings › your name › Subscriptions.
  • If you cancel at least a day before the free trial ends, you are not charged.
  • Without a subscription everything you logged stays readable, and the summary, PDF, CSV export and backup keep working. Adding entries and reminders pause.
  • Refunds are handled by Apple at reportaproblem.apple.com.
