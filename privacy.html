The short version. Acclimate has no account, no server, no analytics and no advertising. What you log stays on your phone. We do not receive it.

Effective 15 November 2026. Published by Hayri Odabaş.

What Acclimate stores, and where

Everything you enter is kept in one file on your phone: the medication name you type, amounts, your schedule, the doses you log and where you injected, how you felt, your notes and your weight.

  • The file is protected by iOS and is encrypted while your phone is locked.
  • The app keeps it out of iCloud and out of your phone's device backups.
  • It is not sent to us or to anyone else.

What we collect

Nothing. The app contains no analytics, no advertising and no third-party code that sends data anywhere. We have no way to see your entries.

One thing that is Apple's rather than ours: if you chose in iOS Settings to share analytics with app developers, Apple gives developers anonymous crash and usage statistics. You can turn that off in Settings › Privacy & Security › Analytics & Improvements.

Apple Health

Sharing with Apple Health is optional and off until you turn it on. Acclimate asks for body weight only.

  • It saves the weights you log in Acclimate to Health.
  • It reads weights logged elsewhere so your summary can include them. They are stored in the same file on your phone.
  • Health data is never used for advertising or marketing, never sold, and never shared with anyone.
  • You can switch sharing off in Acclimate's settings or in the Health app at any time.

Reminders

Reminders are scheduled on your phone, at the time you set. The notification names neither the medication nor the amount.

Subscription

Purchases are handled by Apple. We do not receive your name, e-mail address or payment details. The app only learns from your device whether a subscription is active.

Summaries, exports and backups

A PDF summary, a CSV export or a backup file is created only when you ask for it, and it goes only where you send it. Once a file has left the app, it is in your hands and in the hands of whoever you gave it to.

Deleting your data

You can delete any entry in the app. Deleting the app removes everything it stored. There is nothing to delete on our side, because we hold nothing. For the same reason we cannot look up, export or restore your entries for you: keep a backup file.

Children

Acclimate is intended for adults and is not directed at children.

Not medical advice

Acclimate counts what you log. It does not suggest doses or dates, does not estimate medication levels and does not give medical advice.

Changes and contact

If this policy changes, the new version will be published on this page with a new date.
